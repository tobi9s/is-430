\chapter{Conclusion}

To conclude this report, the lesson is that cybersecurity depends on the security of the entire architecture as well as the organization's efforts to keep their assets secure.
\newline
In this incident, the combination of technical and organizational weaknesses allowed an initial compromise to escalate to the exfiltration of highly sensitive information of over 100 million consumers.
\newline
Therefore, the analysis shows that security controls have to work across processes, applications and infrastructure. Additionally, it also signals that cybersecurity culture must also be strengthened across organizations as even the best technical defenses can be breached if an organization doesn't take them seriously.
