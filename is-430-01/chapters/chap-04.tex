\chapter{Threat Actors and Motivation}
Three individuals have been charged in regard to the incident \cite{doj, wusf}. These individuals are:
\begin{itemize}
\item Graham Ivan Clark, 23, from the US.
\item Nima Fazeli, 27-28, from the US
\item Matthew Sheppard, 24-25, from the UK.
\end{itemize}
All three individuals are simple cybercriminals, meaning that they didn't belong to any criminal organization or state-sponsored group, so they were criminals that followed their personal agenda.
\newline
Although it has not been confirmed, it is possible that their motivation had a purely financial as the attackers used the compromised accounts to lure users to send Bitcoin to a designated address. Additionally, according to Reuters, a gray market site named OGUsers had a sale for Twitter accounts with desirable usernames \cite{reuters}. 
\newline
As stated before, the attackers did not exploit technical vulnerabilities but managed to compromise credentials and accounts through social engineering.
