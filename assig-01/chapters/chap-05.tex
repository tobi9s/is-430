\chapter{Impact of the Incident}
Multiple factors suffered consequences from this incident and are as follows:
\section{Technical Impact}
Although no systems were compromised or had any downtimes, multiple Twitter employee accounts were compromised as the attackers managed to obtain their credentials.
\newline
Even during the incident, Twitter remained active and usable although when Twitter detected the incident, it disabled the ability to send messages or reset passwords for accounts that had the status "verified".
\newline
The attackers targeted 130 Twitter accounts, send messages from 45, private messages from 36 accounts were accessed but not downloaded and data from seven accounts was downloaded.
\section{Business or operational impact}
Fortunately, this incident did not result in any downtime in Twitter's systems, neither did it disrupt any services but verified users were incapable of sending messages or reset their password for a while when the incident was detected.
\newline
There are no sources whether this incident resulted in recovery costs or loss in business opportunities.
\section{Information-security impact}
With regard to the CIA triad, this incident mainly \textbf{confidentiality} as the attackers obtained credentials from Twitter employees, received access to private messages from 45 compromised user accounts, and downloaded data from seven accounts. Additionally, \textbf{integrity} was also affected as the source of the sent messages were not from the actual account owners themselves, but from the attackers. \textbf{Availability} was not affected as Twitter remained accessible during the entire incident.
\section{Organizational and societal impact}
One aspect that was immediately affected by the incident was the financial aspect, as Twitter's stock price went down by four percent after the market closed \cite{techcrunch}.
\newline
Additionally, this incident could have led to reputational damages as the attackers took control of multiple high-profile accounts that had a certain amount of trust by the public.
\newline
Lastly, the incident had an impact of users, who fell for the scam and have sent Bitcoin to the given address as Bitcoin transactions are not able to be undone.
